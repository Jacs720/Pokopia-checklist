## Summary

- Three Pokédex sections: **Main**, **Event**, and **Bubbly Basin DLC**.
- Two independent checklist views: **Pokémon Dex** and **Habitat Dex**.
- Sprite-only Pokémon grid and image-first habitat grid; unchecked entries are visually darkened.
- Complete habitat indexes with **209 Main**, **7 Event**, and **36 Bubbly Basin** entries.
- Language selector for English, Japanese, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
- Information dialog with abilities, registered state, Home, Litter Drop, habitat details, Ideal Habitat, Favorites, and related habitat information.
- Filters for search, checklist status, Ideal Habitat, Litter Drop, Favorite, and Ability.
- Local progress persistence through `localStorage`.
- Separate Pokopia and Bubbly Basin color themes using the supplied palette.
- DLC Favorites gaps are preserved as undocumented instead of being guessed.

Habitat artwork is loaded from Serebii. Localized Pokémon species names come from the PokeAPI data set, while habitat names and criteria are translated on demand and cached in the browser.
